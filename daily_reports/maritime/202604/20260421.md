### MFM-DETR: Misalignment-Free Multimodal DETR for Maritime Object Detection

[原文](https://doi.org/10.1109/icassp55912.2026.11461783) · [PDF]()
作者：Meng Hua Xu, Haitao Zhang, Xicheng Zhang, Sen Jia
发布日期：2026-04-21
主专题：A；专题：A, B；场景：海事智能感知与智能航运
相关性：方法可借鉴 (15)
专题 A: 摘要:cross-modal, 标题:object detection, 摘要:perception, 摘要:alignment；独立概念组=2，分数=12；专题 B: 标题:object detection；独立概念组=1，分数=5；应用优先级加分 +3

Multi-modal object detection is crucial for reliable perception in complex maritime environments. Yet most existing RGB– hyperspectral imagery (HSI) fusion methods overlook cross-modal misalignment, assuming perfect alignment and thus producing unstable features. To address this issue, we develop a detection framework that first employs a kernel-aware modality-adaptive network (KAMN) to extract complementary spatial–spectral features, then uses an offset-guided progressive alignment (OPA) module to refine geometric consistency, and finally applies a modality-guided attention fusion (MGAF) module to integrate features across spatial and semantic levels. Experiments on challenging maritime scenarios demonstrate that our approach surpasses both single-modal and multi-modal baselines, achieving higher accuracy and robustness under weak alignment conditions.
