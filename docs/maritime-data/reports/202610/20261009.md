Title: MarVAIS: A Dataset of Paired Video and AIS Telemetry for Coastal Maritime Monitoring under Adverse Weather Conditions Abstract: This dataset provides synchronized monocular video recordings and Automatic Identification System (AIS) telemetry designed to support maritime domain awareness and object detection research. It is specifically built for the development, validation, and benchmarking of computer vision models (such as YOLO architectures), digital twins, and hybrid tracking systems aimed at identifying collaborative and non-collaborative vessels in coastal regions. Dataset Contents: Due to the large file sizes, the physical dataset is securely hosted on an external cloud (Google Drive). The dataset consists of: Video Data: 40 minutes of continuous footage, divided into 4 individual video sequences of 10 minutes each. All videos are recorded in Full HD (1920x1080 pixels) at 30 frames per second (FPS), yielding approximately 72,000 frames. Each sequence represents distinct environmental and lighting challenges to test model robustness: Clear Weather: Provides high-visibility baselines for accurate bounding box regression and classification. Fog: Challenges contour detection and tracking algorithms due to atmospheric scattering. Rain: Introduces visual artifacts, sensor noise, and partial occlusion of distant targets. Late Afternoon: Tests the impact of fading illumination, shadows, and color space degradation. Synchronized AIS Telemetry: 4 tabular CSV files containing the raw AIS messages corresponding to each video. The timestamps have been converted to a relative video time format (00:00:00) to facilitate immediate synchronization for sensor fusion algorithms. The data includes MMSI, Latitude, Longitude, and Distance. Access Conditions &amp; Request Requirements: Due to the file sizes and the restricted nature of this maritime data, the actual video and CSV files are not available for direct download on Zenodo.
