FedTaste: Topology-Aware Structural Transfer for Multimodal Federated Learning with Missing Modalities

作者：Haochen Liang

第一作者单位：The University of Tokyo, Tokyo, Japan （OpenAlex first authorship）

ACM MM 2026 · CCF A · 2026-11-05

方法值得关注 · AI相关分 28/100 新近收录

论文内容：FedTaste针对多模态联邦学习中客户端模态缺失与非独立同分布数据引发的表征漂移问题，利用共享语义组空间中的可迁移组级语义关系，通过模态自适应结构提示与置信度感知全局蓝图实现参数高效的结构迁移，避免生成式插补与原始数据交换。

迁移思路：研究推断：可将置信度感知全局蓝图与拓扑、谱正则化用于雷达–AIS或视觉–AIS关联中的缺失模态对齐模块，以冻结编码器加可训练提示为输入侧调整、以部分表征对齐为监督，并压缩通信开销。

查看摘要（中文 / English）

多模态联邦学习（MFL）面临客户端级模态缺失和非独立同分布（Non-IID）数据分布问题，这可能引起显著的表征漂移并阻碍有效的跨客户端协作。现有方法通常依赖生成式插补、外部辅助数据或孤立的单模态训练来弥合模态差距，往往带来额外的计算、通信或隐私问题。我们提出FedTaste，一个用于缺失模态下MFL拓扑感知结构迁移的参数高效框架。FedTaste不单纯依赖一阶对齐，而是利用共享语义组空间内可迁移的组级语义关系。全模态客户端使用冻结的视觉-语言编码器和可训练的模态自适应结构提示来蒸馏联合关系结构，服务器将其整合为置信度感知的全局蓝图。缺失模态客户端仅优化其可用分支所附带的提示，并通过拓扑与谱正则化使其部分表征与蓝图对齐。因此，FedTaste在联邦优化过程中避免显式模态插补和原始数据交换，同时保留客户端间共享的语义结构。在标准跨模态检索基准上的实验表明，FedTaste在具有挑战性的Non-IID和模态异构设置下始终优于代表性基线，同时保持良好的通信效率。

Multimodal Federated Learning (MFL) faces client-level modality missingness and Non-IID data distributions, which can induce substantial representation drift and hinder effective cross-client collaboration. Existing approaches commonly rely on generative imputation, external auxiliary data, or isolated unimodal training to bridge modality gaps, often introducing additional computational, communication, or privacy concerns. We propose FedTaste, a parameter-efficient framework for topology-aware structural transfer in MFL with missing modalities. Rather than relying solely on first-order alignment, FedTaste exploits transferable group-level semantic relations within a shared semantic-group space. Full-modality clients use frozen vision-language encoders with trainable Modality-Adaptive Structural Prompts to distill joint relational structure, which the server consolidates into a confidence-aware global blueprint. Missing-modality clients optimize only the prompts attached to their available branches and align their partial representations with the blueprint through topology and spectral regularization. FedTaste therefore avoids explicit modality imputation and raw-data exchange during federated optimization while preserving shared semantic structure across clients. Experiments on standard cross-modal retrieval benchmarks show that FedTaste consistently outperforms representative baselines under challenging Non-IID and modality-heterogeneous settings while maintaining favorable communication efficiency.

Edge Assisted Multi-Camera Vehicle Tracking Framework for Real-Time and Scalable Deployment

作者：Lin, Yuqiang

第一作者单位：University of Bath , United Kingdom （OpenAlex first authorship）

arXiv · 2025-11-17

直接相关 · AI相关分 44/100 新近收录

论文内容：针对多摄像机车辆跟踪实时性与可扩展性不足的问题，提出边-服务器分布式框架EASE-MCVT，通过边端检测跟踪与特征提取、云端跨摄像头关联，实现实时城市级部署。

迁移思路：研究推断：可将其边端轻量元数据上云与服务器重匹配模块迁移至多传感器融合跟踪，在视觉或Radar检测后提取位置与外观特征，仅上传元数据至中心节点，并加入时空约束模型加速跨传感器关联。

查看摘要（中文 / English）

摄像头是现代智能交通系统（ITS）中的核心感知模态，可提供道路使用者活动的丰富视觉信息。多摄像机车辆跟踪（MCVT）利用这些数据在摄像机网络中重建车辆轨迹，支撑交通流预测与优化等应用。然而，现有大多数MCVT研究侧重跟踪精度，而对实时性能和可扩展性关注有限，而这两者对于真实世界和城市级部署都至关重要。为弥补这一空白，我们提出边辅助、可扩展且高效的MCVT（EASE-MCVT），一种面向实时吞吐和可扩展运行的分布式边-服务器框架。在边缘侧，每个摄像机流经过目标检测、单摄像机跟踪、地理映射和特征提取处理，而仅有轻量元数据（包括车辆位置和外观特征）被发送到中央服务器进行跨摄像机关联。为提高跟踪精度和系统效率，EASE-MCVT从算法和系统两个角度进行优化。在算法上，它引入面向轨迹段级特征提取的动态工作量方案、服务器端重匹配模块以重新连接碎片化轨迹段，以及学习时空约束以加速并稳定跨摄像机关联的自监督摄像机链路模型。在系统上，它集成面向生产的数据工程组件，以标准化大规模运行中的部署和数据交换。据我们所知，EASE-MCVT是首个明确设计用于在分布式边-服务器环境中同时解决实时性能和可扩展性的MCVT框架。在RoundaboutHD和CityFlow数据集上的实验展示了实时吞吐和具有竞争力的跟踪精度，为城市级实时交通管理铺平了道路。

Cameras are a core sensing modality in modern intelligent transportation systems (ITS), providing rich visual information on road-user activities. Multi-Camera Vehicle Tracking (MCVT) uses this data to reconstruct vehicle trajectories across camera networks, supporting applications such as traffic flow prediction and optimisation. However, most existing MCVT studies emphasise tracking accuracy while paying limited attention to real-time performance and scalability, both essential for real-world and city-scale deployment. To address this gap, we propose Edge-Assisted, Scalable and Efficient MCVT (EASE-MCVT), a distributed edge--server framework designed for real-time throughput and scalable operation. On the edge side, each camera stream is processed through object detection, single-camera tracking, geo-mapping and feature extraction, while only lightweight metadata, including vehicle locations and appearance features, is sent to the central server for cross-camera association. To improve both tracking accuracy and system efficiency, EASE-MCVT is optimised from algorithmic and system perspectives. Algorithmically, it introduces a dynamic workload scheme for tracklet-level feature extraction, a server-side re-match module to reconnect fragmented tracklets, and a self-supervised camera link model that learns spatio-temporal constraints to accelerate and stabilise cross-camera association. Systemically, it integrates production-oriented data engineering components to standardise deployment and data exchange for large-scale operation. To the best of our knowledge, EASE-MCVT is the first MCVT framework explicitly designed to address both real-time performance and scalability in a distributed edge--server setting. Experiments on the RoundaboutHD and CityFlow datasets demonstrate real-time throughput with competitive tracking accuracy, paving the way for city-wide real-time traffic management.

How Reliable Are Object Detection Models Beyond Clear Weather? A Deployment-Oriented Robustness Study for Autonomous Driving

作者：Munshaibur Rahman Mahin

第一作者单位：待核实

arXiv · 2026-10-15

直接相关 · AI相关分 52/100 近期候选

论文内容：本研究在仅用晴天场景训练、不做适配的条件下，跨雾、雨、雪、夜间测试多种检测器，用性能下降比例、天气鲁棒性指数WRI和ECE表征鲁棒性与置信度可靠性，为自动驾驶部署提供依据。

迁移思路：研究推断：可将仅源域训练后跨天气评测、性能下降与WRI及ECE置信度校准这套流程，迁移到船舶检测与分割的多传感器融合模块，在Radar–AIS或视觉–AIS关联输入上做跨域监督与结构调整。

查看摘要（中文 / English）

自动驾驶感知系统必须在多样环境条件下保持可靠的目标检测，而不仅是在晴朗的日间场景中。然而，在仅源域评估条件下，现代检测器的跨天气鲁棒性仍未得到充分刻画。本研究评估了仅在晴朗日间驾驶场景上训练、且未做任何适配即在未见过的雾、雨、雪和夜间条件下测试的代表性目标检测架构。评估在统一协议下覆盖轻量级和中尺度的一阶段、两阶段以及基于Transformer的检测器。鲁棒性通过性能下降百分比（Drop，%）、天气鲁棒性指数（WRI）以及针对关键道路参与者（包括小汽车、公交车、卡车和行人）的类别级敏感性分析来评估。环境域偏移下的预测置信度可靠性使用期望校准误差（ECE）度量。结果表明在不利条件下性能持续下降，夜间达到62.90%。公交车对环境变化最为敏感，而小汽车在不同天气转换间相对稳定。比较表明，基于YOLO的检测器表现出最低的校准失配，反映出预测置信度与经验检测正确性之间更强的一致性。相比之下，DETR取得最低的鲁棒性得分，WRI为0.6859，表明其更易受跨天气域偏移影响。这些发现为安全关键的智能车辆感知系统的检测器鲁棒性与置信度可靠性提供了面向部署的洞见。

Autonomous driving perception systems must maintain reliable object detection under diverse environmental conditions, not only in clear daytime scenes. However, the cross-weather robustness of modern detectors remains insufficiently characterised under source-only evaluation. This study evaluates representative object detection architectures trained exclusively on clear daytime driving scenes and tested without adaptation under unseen fog, rain, snow, and night conditions. The evaluation covers lightweight and medium-scale one-stage, two-stage, and transformer-based detectors within a unified protocol. Robustness is assessed using performance Drop (%), a Weather Robustness Index (WRI), and class-level sensitivity analysis for critical road participants, including cars, buses, trucks, and persons. Prediction confidence reliability under environmental domain shift is measured using Expected Calibration Error (ECE). Results reveal consistent performance degradation in adverse conditions, reaching 62.90% at night. Buses show the highest sensitivity to environmental variability, while cars remain comparatively stable across weather transitions. Comparisons indicate that YOLO-based detectors exhibit the lowest calibration mismatch, reflecting stronger agreement between predicted confidence and empirical detection correctness. In contrast, DETR achieves the lowest robustness score, with a WRI of 0.6859, indicating greater vulnerability to cross-weather domain shift. These findings offer deployment-oriented insights into detector robustness and confidence reliability for safety-critical intelligent vehicle perception systems.

On the modality gap and the contrastive loss in multi-modal representation learning

作者：Fabian Mager

第一作者单位：Department of Applied Mathematics and Computer Science Technical University of Denmark （OpenAlex first authorship）

arXiv · 2026-07-12

方法值得关注 · AI相关分 32/100 历史补荐

论文内容：本文研究CLIP式双编码器对比学习中的模态间隙，指出InfoNCE在独立编码器下的失效导致图像与文嵌入错位，提出xNCE修改以缩小间隙并保持检索与零样本分类性能。

迁移思路：研究推断：可将xNCE的模态内与模态间负对比对机制迁移至视觉–AIS或Radar–AIS关联的双编码器融合模块，在低温度下构造跨模态与同模态负样本，调整监督对结构以缩小特征间隙并保持判别性。

查看摘要（中文 / English）

我们研究CLIP式双编码器对比学习中的模态间隙，即图像和文本嵌入尽管在共享空间中训练，却仍然保持错位。我们认为该间隙是由独立编码器的InfoNCE公式的失效所诱导的。我们进行了一项单模态实验，使用两个独立编码器和相同初始化条件，发现InfoNCE在低温度下会主动生成间隙。我们对该现象提供了理论分析，并表明模态间隙确实是InfoNCE的一种模式失效，但仅在低温度下发生。我们提出了一种称为xNCE的简单修改，它同时使用模态间和模态内的负对比对。xNCE在MS-COCO上匹配检索性能，同时即使在低温度下也持续减小间隙。值得注意的是，xNCE在所有基准上改进了零样本分类，优于InfoNCE基线，而高温度InfoNCE和正则化InfoNCE均未能做到这一点，这表明xNCE减小了模态间隙，而未牺牲迁移所需的判别几何。

We study the modality gap in CLIP-style dual-encoder contrastive learning, where image and text embeddings remain misaligned despite being trained in a shared space. We argue that the gap is induced by a failure of the InfoNCE formulation with independent encoders. We conduct a uni-modal experiment with two independent encoders and identical initialization conditions and find that InfoNCE actively generates a gap at low temperatures. We provide a theoretical analysis of this phenomenon and show that the modality gap is indeed a mode-failure of InfoNCE, but only at low temperatures. We propose a simple modification called xNCE, which uses intermodal as well as intra-modality negative contrastive pairs. xNCE matches retrieval performance on MS-COCO while consistently reducing the gap even at low temperatures. Notably, xNCE improves zero-shot classification over the InfoNCE baseline across all benchmarks, whereas high-temperature InfoNCE and regularized InfoNCE both fail to do so, demonstrating that xNCE reduces the modality gap without sacrificing the discriminative geometry needed for transfer.

AdNGCL: Adaptive Negative Scheduling for Graph Contrastive Learning

作者：Adnan Ali

第一作者单位：School of Computer Science and Technology , University of Science and Technology of China , Hefei , China （OpenAlex first authorship）

arXiv · 2026-05-04

方法值得关注 · AI相关分 28/100 历史补荐

论文内容：该论文提出AdNGCL框架，将负样本候选池与参与优化的子集分离，利用训练响应信号自适应调度负样本难度，在十个图基准上多数取得最优Micro-F1。

迁移思路：研究推断：可将训练响应驱动的难度调度用于船舶检测中的负样本/困难样本挖掘模块，按检测损失变化动态调整输入候选难度并保持标准对比损失。

查看摘要（中文 / English）

负样本在图对比学习（GCL）中起着关键作用，然而现有方法主要关注应选择、挖掘、过滤或逐步引入哪些负样本。较少关注将锚点可用的负候选与主动参与优化的子集分离，并根据训练行为调整这种暴露。为填补这一空白，我们提出面向图对比学习的自适应负调度（AdNGCL）框架，它为每个锚点维护一个有界候选池，并随着表示演化定期重新评估候选难度。其核心组件——难度感知负调度（HANS）——使用类别特定的训练响应信号来决定在每个调度时机是否应增加对简单、中等或困难负样本的暴露。此外，可靠性感知候选刷新处理可能类似正样本的候选，而类内多样性刷新在不改变HANS控制的暴露的情况下更换活跃身份。AdNGCL保留标准对称InfoNCE目标。在十个基准数据集上针对最先进的自监督图学习方法的实验表明，AdNGCL在八个数据集上在所评估方法中取得最高平均Micro-F1，相比第二优方法提升最多1.30个百分点，在Actor上排名第二，在Coauthor-CS上保持竞争力。消融、敏感性、调度器与可靠性诊断以及计算分析进一步考察了所提出机制的行为和贡献。

Negative samples play a crucial role in graph contrastive learning (GCL), yet existing methods mainly focus on which negatives should be selected, mined, filtered, or progressively introduced. Less attention has been given to separating the negative candidates available to an anchor from the subset that actively participates in optimization, and to adapting this exposure according to training behavior. To address this gap, we propose the Adaptive Negative Scheduling for Graph Contrastive Learning (AdNGCL) framework, which maintains a bounded per-anchor candidate reservoir and periodically reassesses candidate hardness as representations evolve. Its core component, Hardness-Aware Negative Scheduling (HANS), uses a category-specific training-response signal to determine whether exposure to easy, intermediate, or hard negatives should increase at each scheduling opportunity. Furthermore, reliability-aware candidate refresh handles potentially positive-like candidates, while within-category diversity refresh changes active identities without altering HANS-controlled exposure. AdNGCL retains a standard symmetric InfoNCE objective. Experiments on ten benchmark datasets against state-of-the-art self-supervised graph learning methods show that AdNGCL achieves the highest mean Micro-F1 among the evaluated methods on eight datasets, with improvements of up to 1.30 percentage points over the second-best method, ranks second on Actor, and remains competitive on Coauthor-CS. Ablation, sensitivity, scheduler and reliability diagnostics, and computational analyses further examine the behavior and contribution of the proposed mechanisms.
