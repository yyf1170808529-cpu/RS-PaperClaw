Bidirectional Adaptive Transformers for Multimodal Anomaly Detection

作者：Yuxin Jiang

第一作者单位：School of Mechanical Science and Engineering, Huazhong University of Science and Technology, Wuhan, 430000, Hubei, China （OpenAlex first authorship）

arXiv · 2026-10-10

方法值得关注 · AI相关分 47/100 近期候选

论文内容：论文针对多模态异常检测中冻结预训练编码器静态融合导致跨模态干扰的问题，提出双向自适应Transformer框架，通过双向特征对齐与轻量适配器实现语义对齐的多模态表示，在MVTec 3D-AD和Eyecandies上取得最优性能。

迁移思路：研究推断：可将双向互预测对齐模块引入船舶多传感器融合检测，以RGB纹理与LiDAR点云几何互为监督，并在冻结骨干上接入轻量适配器微调，增强视觉–AIS关联中的跨模态语义对应与空间上下文感知。

查看摘要（中文 / English）

RGB图像与3D点云所衍生特征的有效融合，在多模态异常检测（MAD）领域构成重大挑战。现有MAD方法通常依赖建立在冻结预训练编码器之上的静态融合范式。这些框架虽有助于空间对齐，却缺乏建立潜在语义对应关系的机制，从而引入跨模态干扰，削弱异常判别能力。为缓解上述局限，本研究提出双向自适应Transformer（BAT）框架，通过利用跨模态的互补属性来构建全面且语义对齐的多模态表示。BAT框架引入双向特征对齐（BFA）模块，采用相互预测机制弥合2D纹理模式与3D几何结构之间的差距。这种双向交互显式增强了点云描述子的纹理粒度，同时增进了RGB特征的空间上下文感知。为确保潜在表示具备足以支撑对齐的信息密度，BAT纳入参数高效适配架构。通过使用轻量图像与点云适配器，该框架实现对预训练模型的专门微调，减轻冻结编码器固有的信息损失。在MVTec 3D-AD与Eyecandies上进行的大量实验证实，BAT达到最先进的异常检测性能，显著优于现有MAD方法。

The effective integration of features derived from RGB images and 3D point clouds presents a substantial challenge in the field of multimodal anomaly detection (MAD). Existing MAD methods typically rely on static fusion paradigms built upon frozen pre-trained encoders. While these frameworks facilitate spatial alignment, they lack the mechanism to establish latent semantic correspondence, thereby inducing cross-modal interference that degrades anomaly discriminability. To mitigate these limitations, this study proposes a Bidirectional Adaptive Transformers (BAT) framework, which constructs comprehensive and semantically aligned multimodal representations by leveraging complementary attributes across modalities. The BAT framework introduces a Bidirectional Feature Alignment (BFA) module, which employs a mutual predictive mechanism to bridge the gap between 2D textural patterns and 3D geometric structures. This bidirectional interaction explicitly enhances the textural granularity of point cloud descriptors while augmenting the spatial contextual awareness of RGB features. To ensure that the latent representations possess sufficient informational density for alignment, BAT incorporates a parameter-efficient adaptation architecture. By utilizing lightweight image and point cloud adapters, the framework facilitates specialized fine-tuning of pre-trained models, mitigating the information loss inherent in frozen encoders. Extensive experiments conducted on MVTec 3D-AD and Eyecandies confirm that BAT achieves state-of-the-art anomaly detection performance, significantly outperforming existing MAD methods.

ForestQuery: Boundary-Aware and Spatially Anchored Query Learning for Unified Forest Point Cloud Segmentation

作者：Zhihao Zhan

第一作者单位：School of Electronic Science and Engineering , Nanjing University , Nanjing , 210023 , China （OpenAlex first authorship）

arXiv · 2026-10-02

方法值得关注 · AI相关分 47/100 近期候选

论文内容：针对森林点云中结构不规则、遮挡和边界模糊导致的分割难题，提出ForestQuery框架，通过建模边界不确定性和引入空间锚点增强查询学习，统一实现语义与实例分割。

迁移思路：研究推断：可将边界不确定性建模与空间锚点查询增强迁移至船舶点云检测/分割中，利用3D锚点编码垂直分层先验，并以不确定性重加权监督实例查询，适配多传感器融合输入。

查看摘要（中文 / English）

森林点云分割是细粒度三维森林场景理解的基础，但由于树木结构不规则、严重遮挡、密度变化和实例边界模糊而仍具挑战性。近期基于查询的森林分割方法在统一语义与实例预测方面显示出潜力，但它们仍未充分利用森林特有的空间结构，也未考虑边界不确定性。本文提出ForestQuery，一个用于统一森林点云分割的边界感知与空间锚定查询学习框架。ForestQuery通过两个互补设计增强实例与语义查询学习。具体而言，边界不确定性被显式建模，以指导可靠的实例查询构建，并通过自适应损失重加权来调节查询优化。同时，空间锚定语义查询增强（SA-SQE）引入可学习的3D锚点，编码森林垂直分层先验，以显式空间参考丰富语义查询。我们在多个公开森林点云基准和自采集的带标注真实世界数据集上评估ForestQuery。大量实验表明，在不同森林场景中，单木分割和语义分割均取得一致提升。代码和数据公开于https://zhan994.github.io/ForestQuery。

Forest point cloud segmentation is fundamental for fine-grained 3D forest scene understanding, yet remains challenging due to irregular tree structures, severe occlusions, density variations, and ambiguous instance boundaries. Recent query-based forest segmentation methods have shown promise for unified semantic and instance prediction, but they still insufficiently exploit forest-specific spatial structure and account for boundary uncertainty. In this paper, we propose ForestQuery, a boundary-aware and spatially anchored query learning framework for unified forest point cloud segmentation. ForestQuery enhances instance and semantic query learning through two complementary designs. Specifically, boundary uncertainty is explicitly modeled to guide reliable instance query construction and modulate query optimization through adaptive loss reweighting. Meanwhile, spatially anchored semantic query enhancement (SA-SQE) introduces learnable 3D anchors encoding forest vertical stratification priors to enrich semantic queries with explicit spatial references. We evaluate ForestQuery on multiple public forest point cloud benchmarks and a self-collected annotated real-world dataset. Extensive experiments demonstrate consistent improvements in both individual-tree segmentation and semantic segmentation across diverse forest scenes. Code and data are publicly available at https://zhan994.github.io/ForestQuery

EgoRefine: Ego-Referenced Predictive Alignment and Trajectory-Conditioned Reliability-Aware Fusion for Asynchronous Collaborative Perception

作者：Lingzhao Kong

第一作者单位：School of Computer Science and Electronic Engineering , Hunan University , Changsha 410082 , China （OpenAlex first authorship）

arXiv · 2026-09-29

方法值得关注 · AI相关分 47/100 近期候选

论文内容：针对异步协同感知中协作特征延迟到达导致与自车观测残余失配、融合忽略对齐质量空间差异的问题，提出以自车特征引导协作轨迹场预测并对采样偏移做方向精化的补偿方法，结合轨迹条件可靠性感知融合在V2V4Real和DAIR-V2X-Seq上提升3D检测精度。

迁移思路：研究推断：可将自车参考的轨迹场预测与方向偏移精化用于视觉–AIS或Radar–AIS异步关联中的时序对齐模块，以自车/雷达当前帧特征为条件预测AIS目标轨迹并生成采样偏移；融合端以轨迹差异和精化幅度为对齐线索，在卷积融合前对两路特征做条件关系建模与自适应重加权，可迁移至多传感器BEV融合。

查看摘要（中文 / English）

协同感知使联网智能体能够共享互补观测以进行3D目标检测，从而扩展感知范围并缓解遮挡。然而在异步通信下，协作特征会随时间延迟到达。现有基于预测的方法主要依据发送智能体自身的历史来补偿这些特征，因而与自车当前观测之间仍存在残余失配；后续融合也往往忽略对齐质量的空间差异。我们提出EgoRefine，一种面向异步协同感知的自车参考预测对齐与可靠性感知融合框架。其自车参考预测对齐模块利用当前自车特征引导协作轨迹场预测，并沿自车参考轨迹方向精化采样偏移。其轨迹条件可靠性感知融合模块将自车流与协作流之间的轨迹差异以及方向精化幅度作为对齐线索，用它们调节对齐特征之间的关系，并在卷积融合前对两路特征流进行自适应重加权。在V2V4Real和DAIR-V2X-Seq上的实验表明，EgoRefine在AP@0.5和AP@0.7上分别平均超过TraF-Align 1.6和2.9个点。源代码将在https://github.com/godk0509/EgoRefine公开。

Collaborative perception enables connected agents to share complementary observations for 3D object detection, extending sensing range and mitigating occlusion. Under asynchronous communication, however, cooperative features arrive with temporal delay. Existing prediction-based methods compensate for these features mainly from the transmitting agent's own history, leaving residual misalignment with the ego agent's current observation; subsequent fusion also often overlooks spatial variations in alignment quality. We propose EgoRefine, an ego-referenced predictive alignment and reliability-aware fusion framework for asynchronous collaborative perception. Its Ego-referenced Predictive Alignment module uses the current ego feature to guide cooperative trajectory-field prediction and refines the sampling offsets along an ego-referenced trajectory direction. Its Trajectory-conditioned Reliability-aware Fusion module treats the trajectory discrepancy between the ego and cooperative streams and the directional refinement magnitude as alignment cues, using them to condition the relation between aligned features and adaptively reweight the two streams before convolutional fusion. Experiments on V2V4Real and DAIR-V2X-Seq show that EgoRefine outperforms TraF-Align by 1.6 and 2.9 points on average in AP@0.5 and AP@0.7, respectively. The source code will be made publicly available at https://github.com/godk0509/EgoRefine.

Depth-Guided Contrastive Learning for 2D Representations with 3D Spatial Awareness

作者：Liang Zeng

第一作者单位：KU Leuven Belgium （OpenAlex first authorship）

arXiv · 2026-09-23

方法值得关注 · AI相关分 47/100 近期候选

论文内容：论文提出深度引导对比学习DGCL，用相对3D距离比较将局部3D近邻转为对比相似度，为2D对比表示注入空间感知，提升语义下游任务。

迁移思路：研究推断：可将DGCL作为辅助监督接入船舶视觉–AIS或Radar–AIS关联的2D特征提取模块，以深度或传感器几何距离构造像素级正负对约束表示，无需改主干结构。

查看摘要（中文 / English）

标准对比学习框架主要从语义角度设计，但学习能保留3D空间结构的2D视觉表示对场景理解同样重要。本文提出深度引导对比学习（DGCL），一种简单辅助目标，将3D空间感知注入2D对比表示学习。核心思想是利用深度将局部3D邻近性转化为对比相似性：3D空间中更接近的像素被鼓励拥有比更远像素更相似的表示。DGCL不依赖绝对深度值，而是通过随机采样像素间的相对3D距离比较来构建监督，使目标对深度尺度不变、计算高效且易于集成到现有对比框架。跨不同数据集和模型的实验表明，DGCL持续改进2D表示学习，并通过更强的空间与几何理解惠及语义下游任务。代码见https://github.com/LeungTsang/DGCL。

Standard contrastive learning frameworks are mainly designed from a semantic perspective, yet learning 2D visual representations that preserve 3D spatial structure is also important for scene understanding. In this work, we propose Depth-Guided Contrastive Learning (DGCL), a simple auxiliary objective that injects 3D spatial awareness into 2D contrastive representation learning. Our key idea is to use depth to convert local 3D proximity into contrastive similarity: pixels that are closer in 3D space are encouraged to have more similar representations than pixels that are farther apart. Instead of relying on absolute depth values, DGCL formulates supervision through relative 3D distance comparisons among randomly sampled pixels, making the objective invariant to depth scale, efficient to compute, and easy to integrate into existing contrastive frameworks. Experiments across different datasets and models show that DGCL consistently improves 2D representation learning and benefits semantic downstream tasks by stronger spatial and geometric understanding. The code is available on https://github.com/LeungTsang/DGCL.

SALI: Shot-Aware Late Interaction for Cross-Shot Relation Matching in Text-to-Video Retrieval using Film-Grammar Knowledge

作者：Toya Oyama

第一作者单位：⋆ The University of Tokyo （OpenAlex first authorship）

arXiv · 2026-09-24

方法值得关注 · AI相关分 33/100 近期候选

论文内容：针对文本到视频检索中单嵌入丢失人物关系的问题，提出SALI从查询抽取主客体并与各镜头嵌入做贪心最大值或最优传输匹配，借助电影语法惩罚微调，在多镜头关系查询上显著提升R@1。

迁移思路：研究推断：可将镜头感知的晚期交互与主客体文本匹配模块迁移到船舶多镜头检测/分割中，以各镜头视觉特征与主体、客体文本嵌入分别匹配，输入为多镜头视觉嵌入和主客体查询，监督保留原检索损失并加入结构化一致性惩罚。

查看摘要（中文 / English）

文本到视频检索通常用单一嵌入表示一个视频片段。这种嵌入常常丢失人物之间的重要关系。例如，互动“Anna confronts Mark”通常被拍摄为两人的交替镜头和反打镜头（图1a）。任何单一镜头或对片段各镜头取平均的嵌入都无法捕捉这种关系。因此，我们提出SALI（镜头感知的晚期交互）。它从单句查询中提取主语和宾语，并将查询、其主语和宾语的文本嵌入与视频片段的每个视觉镜头嵌入进行匹配。匹配算子为贪心最大值或最优传输。微调中的电影语法惩罚会添加一个小的、一致的偏移。基于CLIP4Clip-meanP构建，SALI在Condensed Movies和ActivityNet上保持整体召回率相当，同时将多镜头关系查询的R@1分别提高3和12个点，在所有对比方法中提升最大，并在MSR-VTT上改进此类查询，代价是整体R@1下降1.4。

Text-to-video retrieval usually represents a video clip by a single embedding. This embedding often loses important relations between people. E.g., an interaction "Anna confronts Mark" is regularly filmed as alternating shot and reverse shot of both (Fig. 1a). No single shot or averaged embedding over clip shots captures this relation. Thus, we propose SALI (Shot-Aware Late Interaction). It extracts the subject and object from a single-sentence query, and matches the query, its subject and object text embeddings against each visual shot embedding of a video clip. The matching operator is greedy max or optimal transport. A film-grammar penalty in fine-tuning adds a small, consistent shift. Built on CLIP4Clip-meanP, SALI keeps overall recall on par on Condensed Movies and ActivityNet while raising R@1 on multi-shot relation queries by 3 and 12 points, the most among all compared methods, and improves such queries on MSR-VTT at a cost of 1.4 R@1 overall.
