MARS and ETMS: A Large-Scale Dataset and Ship-Size Sensitive Detection Method in Maritime Applications

作者：Wen Chen

第一作者单位：Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen （Crossref first author）

IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems · JCR一区 · IF 9.1 · 2026-11

直接相关 · AI相关分 48/100 近期候选

论文内容：该论文提出大规模船舶检测数据集MARS和轻量无锚框网络ETMS，通过重构骨干、自适应加权特征融合与坐标对齐补偿模块，提升中小型船舶检测精度。

迁移思路：研究推断：可迁移至船舶检测的轻量骨干与多尺度特征融合模块，在Radar–AIS或视觉–AIS关联中引入坐标对齐补偿监督，调整检测头与细化损失以提升中小目标关联精度。

查看摘要（中文 / English）

实时且准确的船舶检测是提升海上车辆避碰能力与自主船舶航行安全性的基本要求。本文提出一个实用的大规模船舶检测数据集MARS，通过考虑背景多样性、船舶类别、船舶尺寸、视角变化和海浪等因素，来满足船舶检测任务的数据需求。此外，提出一种名为ETMS（面向海上船舶的增强型训练友好网络）的轻量级无锚框网络，对中小型船舶取得优异检测性能。具体而言，我们首先重构骨干结构，然后在颈部结构中对特征进行自适应加权融合，以轻量级网络提供更丰富的特征表示。其次，提出坐标对齐补偿（CAC）模块，解决头部结构中不同分支最优位置的对齐问题，并设置相应的细化损失以实现一致性优化。我们详尽的实验发现一致证明，ETMS优于其他船舶检测方法，在SeaShips(7000)上达到令人印象深刻的82.7 AP.5:.95，在MARS上达到69.2 AP.5:.95。这些结果凸显了ETMS在船舶检测任务中的有效性。深度学习在海上监视中的应用对于增强海上运输安全和提高港口交通管理效率具有重要意义。

Real-time and accurate detection of ships is an essential requirement to improve marine vehicle collision avoidance and security for autonomous ship navigation. In this paper, we propose a practical large-scale detection dataset for marine ships (MARS), to handle the data requirements of ship detection tasks by considering background diversity, ship categories, ship sizes, viewpoint changes, and marine waves, etc. Furthermore, a lightweight anchor-free network called the enhanced training-time-friendly network (TTFNet) for marine ships (ETMS) is proposed, which achieves excellent detection performance for medium and small sized vessels. In particular, we first reconstruct the backbone structure and then make features perform adaptive weighted fusion in the neck structure to provide more abundant feature representations with a lightweight network. Second, a coordinate alignment compensation (CAC) module is proposed to solve the misalignment problem regarding the optimal location of different branches in the head structure, and the corresponding refinement loss is set to achieve consistency optimization. Our thorough experimental findings consistently prove that ETMS surpasses other ship detection methods, achieving an impressive 82.7 AP.5:.95 on SeaShips(7000) and 69.2 AP.5:.95 on MARS. These results highlight the effectiveness of ETMS for ship detection tasks. The application of deep learning in marine surveillance is of great significance for enhancing marine transportation safety and improving port traffic management efficiency.

NeuroSymbEAD: A Large Scale Neuro-Symbolic Caption Dataset for Omni-Directional Embodied Autonomous Driving

作者：Muhammad Ahmed Ullah Khan

第一作者单位：待核实

arXiv · 2026-09-15

直接相关 · AI相关分 36/100 近期候选

论文内容：本文构建NeuroSymbEAD大规模神经符号描述数据集，在KITTI-360上基于自车中心静态与动态目标知识图谱生成多层级文本描述，为交通场景解释与3D推理提供基准。

迁移思路：研究推断：可将自车中心知识图谱与分层文本描述生成模块迁移至船舶检测/分割，以目标类别、航向与距离为输入构建结构化监督，并用多传感器融合或视觉–AIS关联校验目标关系与位置。

查看摘要（中文 / English）

本文介绍NeuroSymbEAD，一个大规模神经符号描述数据集，其特点是包含以自车为中心的静态和动态目标知识图谱（KG），这些目标标注了类别、范畴、航向方向、朝向以及与自车的距离。这些标注用于KITTI-360数据集，以生成多层级文本描述，表示以自车为中心场景地图的轻量版本。户外场景地图重建、视觉识别和目标定位为驾驶常识和交通/场景理解建立了基准。为此，基于自然语言对目标及其复杂关系进行定位描述，是室内场景任务中广泛采用的上下文表示。神经符号表示已被证明能有效处理各类计算机视觉和语言应用中的结构化信息。我们的数据标注流程允许生成多样化的地图片段，在任意3D目标检测网络预测的边界框内填充模拟或真实目标，并构建分层文本描述。我们使用预训练的定位网络和学习到的自回归描述网络，对我们的神经符号和本体论描述生成进行基准测试。通过将3D驾驶场景转换为结构化的以自车为中心的语言，NeuroSymbEAD为交通场景解释、3D推理和可解释自动驾驶感知的视觉-语言模型和基础模型提供了基准。

This paper introduces NeuroSymbEAD, a large-scale neuro-symbolic caption dataset featuring an ego-centric knowledge graph (KG) of static and dynamic objects annotated with classes, categories, heading directions, orientations, and distances from the ego-vehicle. These annotations are used on the KITTI-360 dataset to generate multilevel textual captions representing a lightweight version of an ego-centric scene map. Outdoor scene-map reconstruction, visual recognition, and object grounding establish baselines for driving common sense and traffic/scene understanding. For these purposes, natural language-based grounded captioning of objects and their complex relationships is a widely adopted contextual representation for indoor scene tasks. Neuro-symbolic representations have proven effective in handling structured information for various computer vision and language applications. Our data annotation pipeline allows the generation of varied map segments, populating simulated or real objects within the bounding boxes predicted by any 3D object detection network, and building hierarchical text captions. We benchmark our neuro-symbolic and ontological caption generation using pre-trained grounding and learned auto-regressive captioning networks. By converting 3D driving scenes into structured ego-centric language, NeuroSymbEAD provides a benchmark for vision-language and foundation models for traffic-scene explanation, 3D reasoning, and interpretable autonomous-driving perception.

Towards Systematic Qualification of Vision-Language Models for Automotive Perception Systems

作者：Malsha Ashani Mahawatta Dona

第一作者单位：] University of Gothenburg and Chalmers University of Technology , Gothenburg , Sweden （OpenAlex first authorship）

arXiv · 2026-09-22

直接相关 · AI相关分 36/100 近期候选

论文内容：论文针对汽车感知中视觉语言模型易产生幻觉的安全风险，提出设计时资格认证工作流，用本体结构化标注与同义词评估在nuScenes上统计量化三种VLM的幻觉，并与运行时监控互补支撑可信部署决策。

迁移思路：研究推断：可将本体结构化标注与同义词评估流程迁移至视觉–AIS关联模块，作为设计时输入校验与监督信号生成机制，并加入目标存在性一致性约束以降低漏检与虚警。

查看摘要（中文 / English）

人工智能已被众多应用领域采用。视觉语言模型是近期进展较快的人工智能技术之一，已被探索用于支持车辆感知、安全保障等汽车功能。然而此类语言模型容易产生幻觉，对可能集成它们汽车系统的安全性构成潜在威胁。在汽车领域，VLM不仅可能幻觉出交通对象，还可能无法识别实际存在的交通对象，这可能导致危险情况。尽管已有越来越多文献提出面向安全可信AI的验证与确认技术，但这些方法往往被孤立研究，仅关注运行时或设计时阶段。此类孤立技术对于汽车感知系统等安全关键的现实场景可能不够充分。本文基于Huang等人提出的分类体系，分析设计时与运行时验证及确认技术。我们呈现一项汽车研究，其中提出一种设计时资格认证工作流以补充运行时监控。该工作流将固定的安全相关本体结构化标注系统与同义词评估过程相结合，以nuScenes数据集数据对三种最先进VLM进行统计评估。我们观察到，所提技术能够确定且可重复地量化VLM在汽车感知相关任务中产生的幻觉。所提工作流支持设计时验证与确认过程中的模型比较和面向部署的工程决策，并将有助于构建追求可信汽车感知系统的整体验证策略。

The field of Artificial Intelligence has been adopted for many application domains. Vision Language Models are one of the recently advanced AI techniques that have been explored to support automotive features such as vehicle perception, and safety assurance. However, such language models are prone to hallucinations, posing a potential threat to the safety of automotive systems that may incorporate them. Within the automotive domain, VLMs could not only hallucinate traffic objects, but could also fail to identify traffic objects that are actually present, which may potentially lead to dangerous situations. Though we have observed a growing body of literature that proposes verification and validation techniques for safe and trustworthy AI, these methods are often studied in isolation, focusing either on run-time or design-time phases. Such isolated techniques could be insufficient in safety-critical, realistic contexts such as automotive perception systems. In this paper, we analyze design-time and run-time verification and validation techniques based on a taxonomy presented by Huang et al. We present an automotive study in which a design-time qualification workflow is proposed to complement run-time monitoring. This workflow combines a fixed safety-relevant ontology-based structured annotation system together with a synonym-based evaluation process to statistically evaluate three state-of-the-art VLMs against data from the nuScenes dataset. We observed that the proposed technique enables deterministic and repeatable quantification of the hallucinations VLMs generate in automotive perception-related tasks. The proposed workflow supports model comparison and deployment-oriented engineering decisions within the design-time verification and validation process and will contribute to a holistic verification strategy that strives towards trustworthy automotive perception systems

Temporal Residual Bottleneck for Robust Asynchronous Collaborative Perception

作者：Melih Yazgan

第一作者单位：FZI Research Center for Information Technology （OpenAlex first authorship）

arXiv · 2026-10-07

直接相关 · AI相关分 40/100 近期候选

论文内容：该论文将异步协同感知建模为时序残差预测，提出以姿态变换特征为锚、Δt条件化xLSTM提取残差并经门控瓶颈校正的方法，在DAIR-V2X和OPV2V上提升严重延迟与丢包下的鲁棒性。

迁移思路：研究推断：可将时序残差瓶颈迁移至多传感器融合中的Radar–AIS或视觉–AIS异步关联模块，以位姿变换的AIS/Radar特征为锚，用延迟条件化xLSTM从历史序列提取残差，经门控校正后再融合，输入为异步特征序列，监督仍用检测或关联标签。

查看摘要（中文 / English）

协同感知扩展了自动驾驶车辆的感知范围，但当共享特征到达过时或不完整时，其性能会下降。大多数对延迟鲁棒的方法通过流引导对齐或直接特征传输来补偿延迟的协作者特征。在本工作中，我们将异步协同感知形式化为时序残差预测。我们的时序残差瓶颈（Temporal Residual Bottleneck）保留一个确定性的姿态扭曲协作者特征作为保守锚点，并使用以$Δt$为条件的xLSTM从可用历史中提取残差时序证据。随后，面向检测器的残差瓶颈在自车侧融合之前仅应用门控的、正则化的校正，从而在时序对应关系不确定时降低覆盖可靠静态结构的风险。在DAIR-V2X和OPV2V上的实验表明，我们的方法在严重的固定/不规则延迟和丢包下尤其有效。在DAIR-V2X上，所报告的检查点在同步峰值精度上牺牲少量以换取在更强通信退化下更好的鲁棒性。受控诊断进一步表明，直接特征传输具有预言机上限空间，但在没有准确对应关系的情况下部署时可能变得不可靠。这些结果支持将时序残差融合作为异步和不完整协同感知的一种实用替代方案。代码将在https://url.fzi.de/8dk38公开。

Collaborative perception extends the sensing range of autonomous vehicles, but its performance degrades when shared features arrive stale or incomplete. Most latency-robust methods compensate delayed collaborator features through flow-guided alignment or direct feature transport. In this work, we formulate asynchronous collaborative perception as temporal residual prediction. Our Temporal Residual Bottleneck keeps a deterministic pose-warped collaborator feature as a conservative anchor and uses a $Δt$-conditioned xLSTM to extract residual temporal evidence from the available history. A detector-facing residual bottleneck then applies only gated, regularized corrections before ego-side fusion, reducing the risk of overwriting reliable static structure when temporal correspondence is uncertain. Experiments on DAIR-V2X and OPV2V show that our method is especially effective under severe fixed/irregular delays and packet drops. On DAIR-V2X, the reported checkpoint trades a small amount of synchronized peak accuracy for better robustness under stronger communication degradation. Controlled diagnostics further indicate that direct feature transport has oracle headroom but can become unreliable when deployed without accurate correspondence. These results support temporal residual fusion as a practical alternative for asynchronous and incomplete collaborative perception. Code will be publicly released at https://url.fzi.de/8dk38.

Trajectory CPHD Filtering for Multiple Turning Vehicles With Decoupled Orientation and Axial-Scale Estimation

作者：Yunhe Cao

第一作者单位：National Key Laboratory of Radar Signal Processing , Xidian University , Xi'an 710071 , China, （OpenAlex first authorship）

arXiv · 2026-09-15

直接相关 · AI相关分 40/100 近期候选

论文内容：该论文针对路口转弯车辆提出轨迹CPHD滤波器与解耦朝向-轴长估计模型，分开建模朝向与半轴长度，并用坐标上升变分推断和固定滞后轨迹校正联合估计车辆朝向、质心与足迹，在仿真和LiDAR数据上提升了转弯时的估计精度。

迁移思路：研究推断：可将解耦朝向与轴长建模嵌入船舶检测/分割中分离航向与船体尺度输出，或以固定滞后轨迹平滑修正视觉–AIS关联中的历史航迹，监督可沿用检测框与点云量测。

查看摘要（中文 / English）

可靠估计车辆朝向、质心和足迹对于表示车辆在交叉口转弯及其他常见道路机动过程中的道路使用者占据状态十分重要。在转弯过程中，车辆朝向与运动方向可能不同，而将朝向与轴向尺度耦合会降低朝向和范围估计的质量。本文提出一种带有解耦朝向与轴向尺度估计（DOAM）模型的轨迹基数化概率假设密度滤波器，用于多车辆跟踪。该模型在每个轨迹分量内分别表示运动学、朝向和半轴长度平方状态，在不引入额外交互多模型结构的情况下减少朝向变化与尺度估计之间的相互干扰。一种结构化的坐标上升变分推断递推联合更新这些状态序列与量测源变量。推导了与解耦表示相关的轨迹分量似然和权重更新，同时保留标准基数递推。固定滞后轨迹实现进一步利用当前量测校正平滑窗口内的历史状态。使用信号化交叉口仿真和真实车载LiDAR量测的评估表明，车辆朝向、质心和范围估计得到改善，尤其是在转弯期间。所得的车辆状态和足迹估计为道路使用者占据感知以及后续碰撞风险评估和运动规划提供信息。

Reliable estimation of vehicle orientation, centroid, and footprint is important for representing road-user occupancy during vehicle turns at intersections and other common road maneuvers. During a turn, the vehicle orientation and direction of motion may differ, and coupling the orientation with the axial scales can degrade both orientation and extent estimates. This paper proposes a trajectory cardinalized probability hypothesis density filter with a Decoupled Orientation and Axial-Scale Estimation (DOAM) model for multiple-vehicle tracking. The model represents the kinematic, orientation, and squared semi-axis-length states separately within each trajectory component, reducing the mutual interference between orientation variation and scale estimation without introducing an additional interacting multiple-model structure. A structured coordinate-ascent variational inference recursion jointly updates these state sequences and the measurement-source variables. The trajectory-component likelihood and weight update associated with the decoupled representation are derived while retaining the standard cardinality recursion. A fixed-lag trajectory implementation further uses current measurements to correct historical states within the smoothing window. Evaluation with signalized-intersection simulations and real onboard LiDAR measurements shows improved estimation of vehicle orientation, centroid, and extent, particularly during turns. The resulting vehicle-state and footprint estimates provide information for road-user occupancy perception and subsequent collision-risk assessment and motion planning.
