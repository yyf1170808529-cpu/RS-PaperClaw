UnfoldCRF: Structured Mask Refinement with Image-Conditioned Latent Regions

作者：Chunming He

第一作者单位：待核实

arXiv · 2026-09-27

方法值得关注 · AI相关分 47/100 近期候选

论文内容：UnfoldCRF将掩码细化建模为像素标签与潜在区域变量上的条件随机场推断，通过展开阻尼平均场更新，在同等参数与监督下比黑箱递归细化器更准且更鲁棒。

迁移思路：研究推断：可将图像条件潜在区域一致性及零状态退出机制迁移到船舶检测/分割的掩码后处理模块，或作为Radar–AIS、视觉–AIS关联中多源证据一致性约束，以输入掩码与图像特征作监督、在关联头后加轻量区域消息传递。

查看摘要（中文 / English）

学习型掩码细化器提高了分割精度，但很难判断其中有多少改进来自显式结构而非额外容量，以及当掩码生成器或其误差分布变化时改进是否依然成立。UnfoldCRF将细化视为像素标签与潜在区域变量上的条件随机场中的推断。其能量包含校正后的一元项、学习到的局部成对交互，以及以图像为条件的潜在区域一致性，并设有一个空状态，使标签一致性较弱的区域可从一致性项中退出；推断是在这一能量上展开的阻尼平均场更新。为分离结构的作用，我们与读取相同输入、获得相同参数预算、阶段数和监督的循环黑箱细化器进行比较。在COD10K上，UnfoldCRF比最强匹配对照高1.0个$F^ω_β$点，改善全部四项COD指标，并将变差图像的比例从11.7\%降至8.5\%。在覆盖五个数据集和多种掩码源的训练一次协议下，2.6M参数变体相对其对照获得4.2平均$Δ$IoU增益，而对照为2.0；基于冻结DINOv2特征的变体以约七分之一的常驻参数匹配最强基础模型细化器，同时保持领先于其自身对照。在训练中从未见过的掩码生成器上，增益为2.0个$F^ω_β$点，而对照为0.6。将各消息单独置零可显示校正来源：成对消息主要修正边界，区域消息主要修正非边界错误。代码与支撑材料将公开发布。

Learned mask refiners improve segmentation accuracy, but it is hard to tell how much of the improvement comes from explicit structure rather than from extra capacity, and whether it holds up when the mask generator or its error distribution changes. UnfoldCRF treats refinement as inference in a conditional random field over pixel labels and latent region variables. Its energy has a corrected unary term, learned local pairwise interactions, and image-conditioned latent-region consistency, with a null state that lets a region with weak label agreement withdraw from the consistency term; inference unrolls damped mean-field updates on this one energy. To isolate the effect of structure, we compare against recurrent black-box refiners that read the same inputs and receive the same parameter budget, stage count, and supervision. On COD10K, UnfoldCRF beats the strongest matched control by 1.0 $F^ω_β$ point, improves all four COD metrics, and lowers the fraction of images made worse from 11.7\% to 8.5\%. Under a train-once protocol over five datasets and several mask sources, the 2.6M-parameter variant gains 4.2 mean $Δ$IoU against 2.0 for its control, and a variant built on frozen DINOv2 features matches the strongest foundation-model refiner with about a seventh of its resident parameters while staying ahead of its own control. On mask generators never seen in training, the gain is 2.0 $F^ω_β$ points against 0.6 for the control. Zeroing individual messages shows where the corrections come from: the pairwise messages mostly fix boundaries, the region messages mostly fix non-boundary errors. Code and supporting materials will be publicly released.

MoVISA: Multi-Token Reasoning for Video Object Segmentation

作者：Ruining Zhao

第一作者单位：待核实

arXiv · 2026-09-24

方法值得关注 · AI相关分 60/100 近期候选

论文内容：论文针对多模态大语言模型视频目标分割中单token（如SEG）定位多个目标粒度不足的问题，提出多token推理框架MoVISA，用SEG0、SEG1等token跨帧表示目标，在MeViS等基准上提升J&F指标。

迁移思路：研究推断：可迁移至视觉–AIS关联与船舶分割模块，将单查询token改为多token分别编码不同船舶目标，在跨帧时序监督下对齐语言提示与掩码预测，输入增加多目标token序列并调整解码头输出。

查看摘要（中文 / English）

近期，基于多模态大语言模型（MLLM）推理的视频目标分割进展已证明，使用单个文本token（如SEG）在图像和视频中预测分割掩码是有效的。然而，我们观察到这种单token策略缺乏在视频分割任务中跨时间精确局部化多个目标所需的粒度。为解决这一局限，我们开发了面向视频目标分割的多token推理方法，即MoVISA。MoVISA使用多个分割token（如SEG0和SEG1）在不同帧中表示一个目标。该设计实现了语言提示与时空掩码预测之间更细粒度的对齐，同时提升了性能和可解释性。在具有挑战性的MeViS、DAVIS17、ReVOS和Ref-Youtube-VOS基准上，我们的模型在MeViS上取得13.2%的J和F提升，在ReVOS上取得8.4%的J和F提升。代码和模型将发布。

Recent advances in video object segmentation with Multimodal Large Language Model (MLLM) reasoning have demonstrated the effectiveness of using a single textual token, such as SEG, to predict segmentation masks across images and videos. However, we observe that this single-token strategy lacks the granularity required to precisely localize multiple objects across time in video segmentation tasks. To address this limitation, we develop Multi-Token Reasoning for Video Object Segmentation, or MoVISA. MoVISA uses multiple segmentation tokens, such as SEG0 and SEG1, to represent an object across different frames. This design enables more fine-grained alignment between language prompts and spatio-temporal mask predictions, improving both performance and interpretability. On the challenging MeViS, DAVIS17, ReVOS, and Ref-Youtube-VOS benchmarks, our model achieves a 13.2 percent J and F improvement on MeViS and an 8.4 percent J and F improvement on ReVOS. Code and models will be released.

Hi-OPD: Hierarchy-Aware Open-Prompt Detection for Remote Sensing Images

作者：Jinlong Hu

第一作者单位：Wuhan University （OpenAlex first authorship）

arXiv · 2026-09-22

方法值得关注 · AI相关分 33/100 近期候选

论文内容：针对多源遥感标注粒度不一致和标签缺失下，平坦开放提示训练无法保证祖先查询时后代目标仍被检索的问题，提出层次感知开放提示检测器Hi-OPD，通过层次安全负采样、路径多正监督和单向向上一致性学习祖先检索，并构建RS153-HierOPD数据集验证。

迁移思路：研究推断：可将层次安全负采样与路径多正监督引入船舶检测的开放提示检测头，以多级类别标签构建祖先-后代监督，并在多传感器融合的特征对齐阶段加入单向向上一致性约束，提升跨粒度检测稳定性。

查看摘要（中文 / English）

Hi-OPD解决了平坦开放提示训练未能控制的一种失败模式：当多源遥感标注呈现不一致的粒度和缺失标签时，后代检索在祖先查询下未必持续。检测器在原子提示下可以定位\textit{car}和\textit{van}，但在\textit{vehicle}下却漏掉相同实例；平坦AP无法暴露这种跨层级不一致。我们提出Hi-OPD，一种层次感知开放提示检测器，并从175,644条保留的训练图像/瓦片记录和3.48M个框构建RS153-HierOPD，映射到153个原子类别，具有稀疏层次和别名关系。Hi-OPD通过层次安全负采样、路径多正监督和单向向上一致性学习祖先检索，同时逐源风险排除处理可能缺失的标签。ConvVPE利用检测器原生特征和共享对比头将K-shot支持框转换为文本兼容嵌入。在Track A上，Hi-OPD在DIOR/DOTA-v2.0上获得79.7/72.3 AP50，高于文献报道的OpenRSD结果76.7/71.8。在原始转换标注的受控训练下，完整层次配方将DOTA-v2.0父类AP50从7.2提升至71.5，FAIR1M祖父类AP50从31.6提升至71.4，而DOTA-v2.0原子AP50从71.4变为72.3。文本路径在三个常见源上达到99.7% CAR50（0.3%违规），在FAIR1M祖父关系上达到99.9%/0.1%。在留出的VEDAI上，文本AP50为75.9，比OpenRSD高6.2个点。联合AP和CAR表明，显式层次训练修复了该失败模式，同时保留了原子检测和提示迁移。

Hi-OPD addresses a failure mode left uncontrolled by flat open-prompt training: descendant retrieval need not persist under ancestor queries when multi-source remote sensing annotations exhibit inconsistent granularity and missing labels. A detector may localize \textit{car} and \textit{van} under atomic prompts yet miss the same instances under \textit{vehicle}; flat AP does not expose this cross-level inconsistency. We propose Hi-OPD, a hierarchy-aware open-prompt detector, and construct RS153-HierOPD from 175,644 retained training image/tile records and 3.48M boxes mapped to 153 atomic categories with sparse hierarchy and alias relations. Hi-OPD learns ancestor retrieval through hierarchy-safe negative sampling, path multi-positive supervision, and one-way upward consistency, while per-source risk exclusion handles potentially missing labels. ConvVPE converts K-shot support boxes into text-compatible embeddings using detector-native features and the shared contrastive head. On Track A, Hi-OPD obtains 79.7/72.3 AP50 on DIOR/DOTA-v2.0, above the literature-reported OpenRSD results of 76.7/71.8. Under controlled training on the original converted annotations, the full hierarchy recipe raises DOTA-v2.0 parent AP50 from 7.2 to 71.5 and FAIR1M grandparent AP50 from 31.6 to 71.4, while DOTA-v2.0 atomic AP50 changes from 71.4 to 72.3. The text path reaches 99.7% CAR50 (0.3% violation) across the three common sources and 99.9%/0.1% on FAIR1M grandparent relations. On held-out VEDAI, text AP50 is 75.9, 6.2 points above OpenRSD. Joint AP and CAR show that explicit hierarchy training repairs this failure mode while retaining atomic detection and prompt transfer.

Needles in a Raystack: Ultra-Sparse LiDAR Occupancy Detection for Bat Tracks

作者：Nico Klar

第一作者单位：待核实

arXiv · 2026-09-17

方法值得关注 · AI相关分 33/100 近期候选

论文内容：论文研究以传感器为中心的LiDAR射线栈中体素级占用检测，用轻量3D U-Net处理蝙蝠夜间飞行造成的超稀疏时空数据，以恢复真实飞行轨迹。

迁移思路：研究推断：可将该轻量3D U-Net与加权BCE加Dice损失迁移至船舶LiDAR点云检测分割模块，用于稀疏船体目标体素占用预测；输入为射线栈，监督为体素占用标签，并保留时间分辨率与跳跃连接以增强小目标细节。

查看摘要（中文 / English）

监测飞行动物对于理解和保护生物多样性十分重要，但蝙蝠等夜行性物种在野外难以观察。利用LiDAR，蝙蝠夜间的运动会产生超稀疏的3D时空数据，标准重建损失往往只预测背景而遗漏真实飞行路径。我们将该问题作为以传感器为中心的LiDAR射线栈中的体素级占用检测来研究。提出一种轻量3D U-Net，它保留时间分辨率，使用跳跃连接获取空间细节，并将加权二元交叉熵与Dice损失结合以处理强类别不平衡。在开阔场地蝙蝠的真实LiDAR记录中，并与声学监测交叉核对，基于重建的3D卷积自编码器基线未能恢复前景轨迹。相比之下，所提出的U-Net在诊断实验中恢复了稀疏前景占用，并沿蝙蝠飞行轨迹产生连贯的占用模式，为验证规模实验、后续飞行轨迹聚类以及未来将蝙蝠活动信息纳入生物多样性感知的风机限电策略提供了实用基础。

Monitoring flying animals is important for understanding and protecting biodiversity, but nocturnal species such as bats are difficult to observe in the field. Using LiDAR, bat movements at night result in ultra-sparse 3D spatio-temporal data in which standard reconstruction losses tend to predict only background and miss real flight paths. We study this problem as voxel-wise occupancy detection in sensor-centric LiDAR raystacks. A lightweight 3D U-Net is proposed that preserves temporal resolution, uses skip connections for spatial detail, and combines weighted binary cross-entropy with Dice loss to handle the strong class imbalance. In real LiDAR recordings of bats over open fields, cross-checked with acoustic monitoring, a reconstruction-based 3D convolutional autoencoder baseline fails to recover foreground trajectories. In contrast, the proposed U-Net recovers sparse foreground occupancy in diagnostic experiments and produces coherent occupancy patterns along bat flight trajectories, providing a practical basis for validation-scale experiments, later clustering of flight tracks, and future integration of bat activity information into biodiversity-aware turbine curtailment strategies.

RegCL: Compact Continual SAM Adaptation for Visual Grounding in Multi-Sensorial Media

作者：Yuan-Chen Shu

第一作者单位：Wangxuan Institute of Computer Technology, Peking University, Beijing, Beijing, China （OpenAlex first authorship）

arXiv · 2026-10-17

方法值得关注 · AI相关分 33/100 近期候选

论文内容：该论文针对多感官媒体中SAM在专业和动态新领域性能下降的问题，提出非回放的持续适应框架RegCL，通过增量模型合并将多域分割知识整合到单个SAM适配器中，实现紧凑的持续视觉分割。

迁移思路：研究推断：可迁移至船舶检测中多传感器融合的Radar–AIS关联模块，以非回放持续学习将雷达与AIS特征适配器增量合并，输入多源BEV特征并监督跨模态一致性，保持紧凑历史统计。

查看摘要（中文 / English）

多感官媒体系统（包括AR/VR、远程操作和具身AI）需要视觉定位模块在感知环境和应用领域演变时保持可靠。Segment Anything Model（SAM）为密集视觉分割提供了强大基础，但其在医学影像、伪装场景和阴影主导环境等专业且动态到达的领域上性能下降。现有持续学习方法通常依赖回放数据或不断增长的领域特定模块，限制了在演变媒体流水线中的紧凑部署。为解决此问题，我们提出RegCL，一种非回放持续适应框架，通过增量模型合并将多域分割知识整合到单个SAM适配器中。RegCL合并轻量级适应模块（例如LoRA风格的AugModules），通过优化合并模型与领域特定适配器之间的预测一致性，同时保留紧凑的历史特征统计。在五个异构分割数据集上的实验表明，RegCL在领域增量学习下实现了强保持和适应，优于有竞争力的非回放持续学习和合并基线。这些结果表明，RegCL可作为演变多感官媒体流水线的紧凑视觉适应组件。代码见https://github.com/Anderw-S/RegCL。

Multi-sensorial media systems, including AR/VR, remote operation, and embodied AI, require visual grounding modules that remain reliable as sensing environments and application domains evolve. The Segment Anything Model (SAM) provides a strong foundation for dense visual segmentation, but its performance degrades on specialized and dynamically arriving domains such as medical imagery, camouflaged scenes, and shadow-dominant environments. Existing continual learning methods often rely on replay data or growing domain-specific modules, limiting compact deployment in evolving media pipelines. To address this issue, we propose RegCL, a non-replay continual adaptation framework that consolidates multi-domain segmentation knowledge into a single SAM adapter through incremental model merging. RegCL merges lightweight adaptation modules, e.g., LoRA-style AugModules, by optimizing prediction consistency between the merged model and domain-specific adapters while carrying forward compact historical feature statistics. Experiments across five heterogeneous segmentation datasets show that RegCL achieves strong retention and adaptation under domain-incremental learning, outperforming competitive non-replay continual learning and merging baselines. These results suggest that RegCL can serve as a compact visual adaptation component for evolving multi-sensorial media pipelines. The code is available at https://github.com/Anderw-S/RegCL
