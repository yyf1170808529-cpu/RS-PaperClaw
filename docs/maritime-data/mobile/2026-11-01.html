CrossWeave: Cross-Modal Scene and Instance Retrieval from Sparse 2D-3D Alignment

作者：Aadith Warrier

第一作者单位：待核实

arXiv · 2026-10-29

方法值得关注 · AI相关分 47/100 近期候选

论文内容：CrossWeave面向由2D图像检索对应3D场景或物体的跨模态任务，提出仅用稀疏2D-3D对应点监督学习patch-点对齐，并以最优传输聚合器聚类冗余区域，单模型在ScanNet和3RScan的场景与实例检索上超越需分任务建模的现有方法。

迁移思路：研究推断：可将稀疏2D-3D对应监督用于船舶视觉–LiDAR或视觉–Radar–AIS关联模块，以patch-点对齐替代场景级对比目标，并引入最优传输聚合器压缩海面冗余区域；输入为图像patch与点云或BEV点，监督为稀疏对应，骨干与聚合头可共享以同时服务检测与分割预训练。

跨模态场景与实例检索旨在给定查询2D图像时检索对应的3D环境或物体，是机器人与AR/VR中空间推理的基础机制。现有方法仅在整个场景或物体层面学习对齐来实现这一目标，这对其内部细粒度局部结构不提供监督。在本工作中，我们表明，对一组稀疏2D-3D对应关系进行监督，而无需场景级或实例级对比目标，就足以训练一个单一模型同时用于场景级和实例级检索。具体而言，我们提出$\textbf{CrossWeave}$，一种用于跨模态检索的新框架，它学习稀疏patch-点对齐，仅此一项就优于现有方法。在对齐特征上使用基于最优传输的聚合器，通过聚类结构冗余区域进一步带来增益，为语义上不同的局部特征释放描述子容量。CrossWeave在ScanNet和3RScan上以$\textit{单一模型}$在场景级和实例级检索上均达到最先进性能，而现有方法需要为每个任务使用单独模型。我们的结果表明，全场景编码对于跨模态检索并非必要，稀疏局部对应监督是一种更有效且灵活的替代方案。

Cross-modal scene and instance retrieval aims to retrieve a corresponding 3D environment or object given query 2D images, serving as a foundational mechanism for spatial reasoning in robotics and AR/VR. Existing methods achieve this by learning alignment only at the level of whole scenes or objects, which provides no supervision for the fine-grained local structure within them. In this work, we show that supervision on a set of sparse 2D-3D correspondences, without scene- or instance-level contrastive objectives, is sufficient to train a single model for both scene- and instance-level retrieval. Specifically, we propose $\textbf{CrossWeave}$, a novel framework for cross-modal retrieval that learns sparse patch-point alignment, which alone outperforms existing methods. An optimal transport-based aggregator over the aligned features gives further gains by clustering structurally redundant regions, freeing descriptor capacity for semantically distinct local features. CrossWeave achieves state-of-the-art performance on both scene- and instance-level retrieval on ScanNet and 3RScan with a $\textit{single model}$, whereas existing methods require separate models for each task. Our results suggest that whole-scene encoding is unnecessary for cross-modal retrieval and that sparse local correspondence supervision is a more effective and flexible alternative.

CFUNet: An Adaptive RGB–Depth Cross-Modal Fusion Network for Pavement Crack Segmentation

作者：Mike Chen

第一作者单位：Jiangsu Second Normal University （OpenAlex first authorship）

Research Square · 2026-10-16

论文内容：本文针对路面裂缝分割中RGB方法对光照敏感、RGB–Depth融合不充分的问题，提出中期融合的双分支CFUNet网络，以实现准确且高效的路面裂缝自动分割。

迁移思路：研究推断：可将EWF自适应加权融合与LFVit轻量全局建模迁移至船舶RGB–深度或视觉–雷达融合检测的分支融合与主干模块，以两模态特征为输入、分割或检测标签为监督，保留双分支结构。

查看摘要（中文 / English）

准确的路面裂缝分割对于智能道路巡检和预防性路面养护至关重要。然而，现有的基于RGB的语义分割方法对光照变化高度敏感，而传统的RGB–Depth融合策略往往无法有效利用外观与几何信息的互补性。为解决这些局限，本文提出CFUNet，一种基于中期融合策略的新型双分支RGB–Depth语义分割框架。该网络采用两个独立的ResNet50编码器分别从RGB图像和深度图中提取模态特定特征。引入增强加权融合（EWF）模块进行自适应跨模态特征聚合，同时轻量融合视觉Transformer（LFVit）以较低计算复杂度增强全局语义表示。此外，设计了频率感知上采样（FAUpSample）模块，以在解码器重建过程中保留高频边界信息。在公开RGB–Depth路面裂缝数据集上的大量实验表明，所提方法在Precision、Recall、F1-score和Intersection over Union（IoU）方面始终优于代表性的基于CNN、基于Transformer和多模态分割网络。消融研究进一步验证了各提出模块的有效性，并表明它们的协同优化显著提升了分割性能。所提CFUNet为智能路面裂缝分割提供了一种准确且计算高效的解决方案，并在实际道路巡检系统部署中展现出可观潜力。

Abstract Accurate pavement crack segmentation is essential for intelligent road inspection and preventive pavement maintenance. However, existing RGB-based semantic segmentation methods are highly sensitive to illumination variations, while conventional RGB–Depth fusion strategies often fail to effectively exploit complementary appearance and geometric information. To address these limitations, this paper proposes CFUNet, a novel dual-branch RGB–Depth semantic segmentation framework based on a mid-level fusion strategy. The proposed network adopts two independent ResNet50 encoders to extract modality-specific features from RGB images and depth maps. An Enhanced Weighted Fusion (EWF) module is introduced to perform adaptive cross-modal feature aggregation, while a Lightweight Fusion Vision Transformer (LFVit) enhances global semantic representation with reduced computational complexity. Furthermore, a Frequency-aware Upsampling (FAUpSample) module is designed to preserve high-frequency boundary information during decoder reconstruction. Extensive experiments on a public RGB–Depth pavement crack dataset demonstrate that the proposed method consistently outperforms representative CNN-based, Transformer-based, and multi-modal segmentation networks in terms of Precision, Recall, F1-score, and Intersection over Union (IoU). Ablation studies further verify the effectiveness of each proposed module and demonstrate that their collaborative optimization substantially improves segmentation performance. The proposed CFUNet provides an accurate and computationally efficient solution for intelligent pavement crack segmentation and shows considerable potential for deployment in practical road inspection systems.

Robust RGB-IR Object Detection under Missing-Modality Conditions via Complementary Feature Restoration and Cross-Modal Fusion

作者：Zhen-Yu Wu

第一作者单位：National Kaohsiung University of Science and Technology （OpenAlex first authorship）

Research Square · 2026-10-07

方法值得关注 · AI相关分 53/100 近期候选

论文内容：该论文针对RGB-IR目标检测在传感器故障导致模态缺失时性能下降的问题，提出双流骨干结合互补特征维护模块与改进跨模态Transformer的框架，在FLIR和LLVIP数据集上验证了完整与缺失模态条件下的鲁棒检测能力。

迁移思路：研究推断：可将互补特征维护模块用于船舶多传感器融合检测中雷达或视觉分支缺失时的特征重建，以通道与分组注意力恢复退化响应，并将Top-k相似度跨模态注意力嵌入视觉–AIS关联模块以缓解异构语义冲突。

查看摘要（中文 / English）

摘要：在具有挑战性的视觉条件下进行目标检测仍然是计算机视觉和实际机器视觉应用中的一个关键问题。可见光RGB图像在低照度、遮挡、恶劣天气或环境干扰下往往会出现严重的性能下降，而红外（IR）图像能够提供与RGB信息互补的稳定热响应。然而，大多数现有的RGB-IR目标检测方法假设两种模态在训练和推理期间都完全可用，而对由传感器故障、图像退化或传输不稳定引起的缺失模态情况的关注相对有限。为解决这一实际限制，本研究提出了一种对缺失模态具有鲁棒性的RGB-IR目标检测框架，该框架能够在完整模态和缺失模态条件下运行。所提出的框架采用双流骨干网络，并引入互补特征维护（CFM）模块，该模块由局部通道注意力、全局分组注意力和特征细化组件组成，用于恢复退化或缺失的模态响应。进一步采用改进的跨模态Transformer（CT）模块，通过空间缩减注意力和Top-k相似度注意力来增强跨模态特征交互并减少异构RGB与IR表示之间的语义冲突。在FLIR数据集上的实验表明，所提出的方法在完整模态输入下达到80.2%的mAP50。在缺失模态条件下，当一半样本在缺失RGB输入下评估、另一半在缺失IR输入下评估时，它保持73.5%的mAP50；当IR完全不可用时，仍达到63.7%。在LLVIP数据集上的额外实验进一步证实，所提出的框架在低光照行人检测场景中保持稳定的检测性能。这些结果表明，所提出的框架在各种缺失模态条件下提供了鲁棒的检测性能，适用于实际的RGB-IR机器视觉应用。

Abstract Object detection under challenging visual conditions remains a critical issue in computer vision and practical machine vision applications. Visible-light RGB images often suffer from severe performance degradation under low illumination, occlusion, adverse weather, or environmental interference, whereas infrared (IR) images can provide stable thermal responses that are complementary to RGB information. However, most existing RGB-IR object detection methods assume that both modalities are fully available during training and inference, and relatively limited attention has been paid to missing-modality situations caused by sensor failure, image degradation, or transmission instability. To address this practical limitation, this study proposes a missing-modality-resilient RGB-IR object detection framework that can operate under both complete- and missing-modality conditions. The proposed framework adopts a dual-stream backbone and introduces a Complementary Feature Maintenance (CFM) module, which consists of local channel attention, global group attention, and feature refinement components to restore degraded or missing modality responses. An improved Cross-modal Transformer (CT) module is further employed to enhance cross-modal feature interaction and reduce semantic conflicts between heterogeneous RGB and IR representations through spatial-reduction attention and Top-k similarity attention. Experiments on the FLIR dataset show that the proposed method achieves an mAP50 of 80.2% with complete-modality input. Under missing-modality conditions, it maintains an mAP50 of 73.5% when half of the samples are evaluated with missing RGB input and the other half with missing IR input, and still reaches 63.7% when IR is completely unavailable. Additional experiments on the LLVIP dataset further confirm that the proposed framework maintains stable detection performance in low-light pedestrian detection scenarios. These results demonstrate that the proposed framework provides robust detection performance under various missing-modality conditions and is suitable for practical RGB-IR machine vision applications.

A Lightweight Feature Recalibration Strategy for Fourier Contour Regression in Remote Sensing Object Detection

作者：Hongyun Zhang

第一作者单位：the University of Emergency Management （OpenAlex first authorship）

论文内容：该论文针对遥感目标检测中傅里叶轮廓回归精度受特征质量影响的问题，在系数回归分支中引入轻量特征重校准模块，以增强边界与形状相关通道并提升检测mAP与AP50。

迁移思路：研究推断：该分支内轻量通道重校准思想可迁移到船舶检测/分割的轮廓或掩码头中，作为回归前的通道注意力模块，输入为多尺度特征、监督为边界点或掩膜，不改变骨干与主检测头。

查看摘要（中文 / English）

摘要 准确的几何表示在遥感目标检测中始终是一个持续存在的挑战，因为目标往往在尺度和方向上变化，其边界可能被杂乱的背景削弱。傅里叶轮廓回归是描述闭合目标形状的一种高效方式，但其准确性依赖于系数回归分支的特征质量。我们提出了一种用于傅里叶轮廓回归的轻量特征重校准策略。所提模块不改变骨干网络、损失函数或整个检测头，而是仅放置在傅里叶系数回归分支中，在其中自适应地强调与边界和形状估计更相关的通道。该方法在iSAID、HRSC2016和UCAS-AOD上采用与Fourier Series Modelling基线相同的设置进行评估。在iSAID上，它将mAP从67.02%提升至68.54%；在HRSC2016上，将AP50从91.60%提升至95.57%；参数和FLOPs仅有可忽略的增加。在基线性能已接近饱和的UCAS-AOD上，该方法保持稳定，并产生了从97.25%到97.37% mAP的小幅增益。结果表明，有针对性的特征重校准可以在不牺牲效率的情况下增强遥感目标检测中基于傅里叶的几何表示。

Abstract Accurate geometric representation remains a persistent challenge in remote sensing object detection, where targets often vary in scale and orientation and their boundaries can be weakened by cluttered backgrounds. Fourier contour regression is an efficient way to describe closed object shapes, but its accuracy depends on the feature quality of the coefficient regression branch. We present a lightweight feature recalibration strategy for Fourier contour regression. Rather than changing the backbone, loss function, or the whole detection head, the proposed module is placed only in the Fourier coefficient regression branch, where it adaptively emphasizes channels that are more relevant to boundary and shape estimation. The method was evaluated on iSAID, HRSC2016, and UCAS-AOD using the same settings as the Fourier Series Modelling baseline. It improved mAP from 67.02% to 68.54% on iSAID and AP50 from 91.60% to 95.57% on HRSC2016, with only negligible increases in parameters and FLOPs. On UCAS-AOD, where baseline performance was already close to saturation, the method remained stable and produced a small gain from 97.25% to 97.37% mAP. The results suggest that targeted feature recalibration can strengthen Fourier-based geometric representation for remote sensing object detection without sacrificing efficiency.

Online Target-less Radar-LiDAR-Camera Extrinsic Calibration via Joint Optimization

作者：Gunhee Shin

第一作者单位：School of Electrical Engineering , KAIST (Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology) , Daejeon , 34141 , Korea （OpenAlex first authorship）

arXiv · 2026-10-03

论文内容：该论文针对雷达-激光雷达-相机系统在线无靶标外参标定中两两组合不一致与雷达稀疏噪声问题，提出联合优化各传感器对残差的标定框架，并引入自适应雷达噪声滤波与对应累积策略，在自采城市数据集上降低各传感器对标定误差。

迁移思路：研究推断：可将联合残差优化框架迁移至船舶多传感器融合的Radar–AIS与视觉–AIS关联模块，以距离相关裕度滤波和跨帧对应累积处理稀疏雷达/AIS观测，并调整关联监督与优化结构以提升跨模态一致性。

查看摘要（中文 / English）

融合雷达、LiDAR和相机可在多样和恶劣条件下实现鲁棒感知，但融合性能关键取决于三个传感器之间精确的外参标定。本文研究雷达-LiDAR-相机系统的在线无靶标外参标定问题。现有无靶标方法大多针对单一传感器对设计，组合两两结果不能保证三个传感器之间的一致性。此外，稀疏且含噪的雷达测量使涉及雷达的传感器对不可靠。为应对这些挑战，我们提出一个联合标定框架，为每个传感器对构建残差，并联合优化所有对的外参以最小化整体残差。此外，我们引入自适应雷达噪声滤波器，利用距离相关裕度拒绝虚假雷达回波，以及一种对应累积策略，跨帧聚合稀疏雷达对应。我们在涵盖多样城市环境的自采雷达-LiDAR-相机数据集上验证了我们的方法，相比最先进的相机-LiDAR基线，该方法降低了所有传感器对的标定误差。

Fusing radar, LiDAR, and camera enables robust perception in diverse and adverse conditions, but the fusion performance critically depends on accurate extrinsic calibration among the three sensors. In this paper, we address the problem of online target-less extrinsic calibration for the radar-LiDAR-camera system. Existing target-less methods are mostly designed for a single sensor pair, and composing the pairwise results does not guarantee consistency across the three sensors. Moreover, the sparse and noisy radar measurements make the radar-involving pairs unreliable. To tackle these challenges, we propose a joint calibration framework that constructs residuals for each sensor pair and optimizes the extrinsics of all pairs together to minimize the overall residual. Furthermore, we introduce an adaptive radar noise filter that rejects spurious radar returns using a range-dependent margin, and a correspondence accumulation strategy that aggregates sparse radar correspondences over frames. We validate our method on an in-house radar-LiDAR-camera dataset covering diverse urban environments, where it reduces calibration errors across all sensor pairs over a state-of-the-art camera-LiDAR baseline.
