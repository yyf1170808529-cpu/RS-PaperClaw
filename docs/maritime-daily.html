CLIP-SPSE: Fine-Grained Semantic Perturbation for Hard Negative Learning in Cross-Modal Retrieval

作者：S. F. Zhang

第一作者单位：Institute of Information Engineering, Chinese Academy of Sciences, Beijing, China and School of Cyber Security, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, Beijing, China （OpenAlex first authorship）

ACM MM 2026 · CCF A · 2026-11-09

方法值得关注 · AI相关分 48/100 新近收录

论文内容：针对CLIP在高全局相似度下难以捕捉细粒度语义差异的问题，提出CLIP-SPSE框架，通过细粒度语义元素扰动生成难负样本并结合语义增强对比编码，提升跨模态检索性能。

迁移思路：研究推断：可将三元组细粒度语义元素扰动与多模型集成验证的难负样本生成模块迁移至视觉–AIS关联或Radar–AIS融合，在特征对齐损失中构造语义近邻难负样本，并引入加权相似度约束监督。

查看摘要（中文 / English）

尽管视觉-语言预训练模型取得了显著进展，但CLIP等现有方法在高全局相似度下仍难以捕捉细粒度语义差异，限制了跨模态检索性能。现有方法通常通过语义操纵构建难负样本，但这些负样本往往与正样本过于可分，因而不足以学习细微的判别线索。为解决这一问题，我们提出CLIP-SPSE，一种通过双重优化增强跨模态表示学习的新框架：细粒度语义元素扰动负样本生成与语义增强对比编码。我们认为有效的难负样本应同时满足负性和难性。在这些假设下，我们使用细粒度语义元素三元组构建具有挑战性的负样本。具体而言，LLM生成扰动负文本，由多LLM集成验证其语义合理性和判别难度，同时图像编辑模型在加权相似度约束下合成视觉负样本，以在保持负性的同时最小化对全局语义的扰动。基准上的大量实验表明，CLIP-SPSE显著提升了跨模态检索。

Despite remarkable progress in vision-language pretrained models, existing approaches such as CLIP still struggle to capture fine-grained semantic differences under high global similarity, limiting cross-modal retrieval performance. Existing methods typically construct hard negatives through semantic manipulation, yet these negatives are often overly separable from positives and thus insufficient for learning subtle discriminative cues. To address this issue, we propose CLIP-SPSE, a novel framework that enhances cross-modal representation learning via dual optimization: fine-grained semantic element-perturbed negative generation and semantic-enhanced contrastive encoding. We argue that effective hard negatives should satisfy both negativeness and hardness. Under these assumptions, we construct challenging negatives using triplets of fine-grained semantic elements. Specifically, an LLM generates perturbed negative texts, which are validated by a multi-LLM ensemble for semantic plausibility and discriminative hardness, while an image editing model synthesizes visual negatives under weighted similarity constraints to preserve negativeness with minimal perturbation to global semantics. Extensive experiments on benchmarks demonstrate that CLIP-SPSE significantly improves cross-modal retrieval.

A Survey of Small Sea-Surface Target Detection for Maritime Search and Rescue

作者：Jianchuan Yin

第一作者单位：Naval Architecture and Shipping College, Guangdong Ocean University, Zhanjiang, China （Crossref first author）

IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems · JCR一区 · IF 9.1 · 2026-01

直接相关 · AI相关分 68/100 历史补荐

论文内容：该综述系统梳理海上搜救中小海面目标检测的深度学习方法、关键挑战与代表性数据集，为提升复杂海面环境下小目标检测性能提供研究方向。

迁移思路：研究推断：其上下文信息与多尺度学习模块可迁移至船舶检测/分割的视觉–AIS关联前端，多尺度特征与注意力机制用于融合Radar–AIS与视觉输入，并以超分辨率或样本导向策略调整小目标监督。

查看摘要（中文 / English）

小海面目标的检测在海上搜救（SAR）行动中发挥着关键作用，保障海上人员与财产安全。本文对面向海上SAR任务的小海面目标检测的最新进展与研究进行了全面综述。基于深度学习的模型通过将图像或视频帧转换为高维抽象表示，实现准确的目标检测与定位，从而在复杂海面环境中实现有效检测。然而，海面上的遮挡、模糊和反射等挑战使小目标检测显著复杂化。为应对这些挑战，本文总结了一系列有效方法，包括上下文信息、多尺度学习、无锚框检测、超分辨率、注意力机制和面向样本的方法。这些方法旨在提升无人飞行器（UAV）和无人补给船等应用中小目标检测的性能。此外，本文对小目标数据集进行了分类，基于其采集方法和应用场景提供了详细概述，并重点介绍了代表性数据集。通过对方法与数据集的深入分析，本文为海上搜救行动中小目标检测技术的未来发展提供了有价值的见解和方向。

The detection of small surface targets plays a critical role in maritime search and rescue (SAR) operations, ensuring the safety of people and property at sea. This paper provides a comprehensive review of the latest advancements and research in small sea surface target detection for maritime SAR missions. Deep learning-based models facilitate accurate target detection and localization by transforming image or video frames into high-dimensional abstract representations, enabling effective detection in complex sea surface environments. However, challenges such as occlusion, blurring, and reflections on the sea surface significantly complicate small target detection. To address these challenges, this paper summarizes a range of effective approaches, including context information, multi-scale learning, anchor-free detection, super-resolution, attention mechanisms, and sample-oriented approaches. These approaches aim to enhance the performance of small target detection in applications such as uncrewed aerial vehicles (UAV) and uncrewed supply vessels. Furthermore, this paper classifies small target datasets, providing a detailed overview based on their collection methods and application scenarios, while highlighting representative datasets. Through a thorough analysis of both methodologies and datasets, this paper offers valuable insights and directions for the future development of small target detection technology in maritime search and rescue operations.

A Novel Approach for Monitoring Vessel Load Capacity Through Ship Dimension Extraction From Synthetic Aperture Radar Images

作者：Di Zhang

第一作者单位：State Key Laboratory of Maritime Technology and Safety, the Intelligent Transportation Systems Research Center, and the School of Transportation and Logistics Engineering, Wuhan University of Technology, Wuhan, China （Crossref first author）

IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems · JCR一区 · IF 9.1 · 2025-12

直接相关 · AI相关分 68/100 历史补荐

论文内容：该论文针对仅靠AIS难以监管关闭设备或未装AIS船舶的问题，提出利用SAR图像经实例分割提取船舶尺寸，再通过多元回归预测载重吨位，实现水域智能监管。

迁移思路：研究推断：可将SAR实例分割提取船体尺寸并回归载重吨位的思路迁移至船舶检测/分割任务，以尺寸为监督信号，融合Radar与视觉或AIS关联模块，调整多任务输出头增强鲁棒性。

查看摘要（中文 / English）

船舶被规定须根据其类型、尺寸、载运能力等在指定水域内运营。然而，尽管有相关规定，一些船东仍试图通过伪造证书、隐瞒船舶信息或未经授权的非法行为来规避规定，以降低运营成本或获取额外利益，这对海上交通安全构成重大威胁。仅通过自动识别系统（AIS）数据进行监测存在局限，因为它依赖于船舶主动提交信息，无法追踪故意关闭AIS或未配备AIS设备的船舶。本文提出一种智能水路运输监管方法，利用合成孔径雷达（SAR）遥感技术自动检测船舶目标并预测其载重吨位（DWT）。通过基于SAR海洋图像的实例分割模型识别船舶目标并提取其主要尺寸。随后，多元回归分析建立船舶类型、尺寸与DWT之间的关系，有效预测其载运能力。实验结果表明，三种主要船型（即集装箱船、散货船、油轮）的平均绝对百分比误差（MAPE）分别为4.99%、6.09%和4.20%。所提方法可作为繁琐传统人工方法的辅助，能够大规模、批量地自动检测船舶及其载运能力，无论船舶是否在港。该方法可应用于船舶航线调度和航道布局，作为避免检查疏漏的额外参考，并显著提升海事监管效率。

Vessels are regulated to operate within designated water areas based on their types, dimensions, load-carrying capacity, etc. However, despite regulations, some ship operators still attempt to evade them to reduce operational costs or obtain additional benefits. This is achieved through certificate forgery, vessel information concealing or unauthorized illegal behavior, which poses a significant threat to maritime traffic safety. Monitoring through only Automatic Identification System (AIS) data has limitations, as it relies on vessels actively submitting information, and is unable to track vessels intentionally shutting down AIS or those without AIS equipment. This paper proposes a method for intelligent waterway transportation supervision that utilizes Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) remote sensing technology to automatically detect vessel targets and predict their Deadweight Tonnage (DWT). Vessel targets are identified and their main dimensions are extracted through instance segmentation models based on SAR marine images. Subsequently, a multiple regression analysis establishes the relationship between vessel type, dimensions, and DWT, effectively predicting their load capacity. The experimental results show that the Mean Absolute Percentage Error (MAPE) for the three primary types of ships (i.e., container ship, bulk carrier, tanker) are 4.99%, 6.09%, and 4.20%, respectively. The proposed method serves as an auxiliary to laborious traditional manual methods, which can automatically detect vessels and their load capacity on a large scale and in batches, regardless of whether the ships are in port or not. It can be applied in scheduling vessel routes and channel layouts, serving as an additional reference to avoid inspection oversights, and significantly enhance maritime regulatory efficiency.

UniTrack: Differentiable Graph Representation Learning for Multi-Object Tracking

作者：Bishoy M. Galoaa

第一作者单位：Augmented Cognition Lab (ACLab) , Electrical and Computer Engineering Department , Northeastern University （OpenAlex first authorship）

arXiv · 2026-02-04

方法值得关注 · AI相关分 28/100 历史补荐

论文内容：UniTrack提出一种即插即用的图论损失函数，通过统一可微学习直接优化跟踪目标，将检测精度、身份保持和时空一致性集成到端到端损失中，在不修改架构的情况下提升多种MOT模型的性能。

迁移思路：研究推断：可将UniTrack的图表示损失函数迁移至船舶多目标跟踪任务，结合Radar与AIS数据构建跨模态图结构，在检测与身份关联模块中引入时空一致性监督，提升船舶身份保持能力。

查看摘要（中文 / English）

我们提出UniTrack，一种即插即用的图论损失函数，旨在通过统一的可微学习直接优化跟踪特定目标，从而显著提升多目标跟踪（MOT）性能。与先前重新设计跟踪架构的基于图的MOT方法不同，UniTrack提供了一种通用的训练目标，将检测精度、身份保持和时空一致性集成到一个端到端可训练的损失函数中，无需架构修改即可与现有MOT系统无缝集成。通过可微图表示学习，UniTrack使网络能够学习跨帧运动连续性和身份关系的整体表示。我们在多种跟踪模型和多个具有挑战性的基准上验证了UniTrack，在包括Trackformer、MOTR、FairMOT、ByteTrack、GTR和MOTE在内的所有测试架构和数据集上均表现出持续改进。大量评估表明，在具有挑战性的基准上，身份切换最多减少53%，IDF1提升12%，其中GTR在SportsMOT上实现了9.7%的MOTA峰值性能提升。

We present UniTrack, a plug-and-play graph-theoretic loss function designed to significantly enhance multi-object tracking (MOT) performance by directly optimizing tracking-specific objectives through unified differentiable learning. Unlike prior graph-based MOT methods that redesign tracking architectures, UniTrack provides a universal training objective that integrates detection accuracy, identity preservation, and spatiotemporal consistency into a single end-to-end trainable loss function, enabling seamless integration with existing MOT systems without architectural modifications. Through differentiable graph representation learning, UniTrack enables networks to learn holistic representations of motion continuity and identity relationships across frames. We validate UniTrack across diverse tracking models and multiple challenging benchmarks, demonstrating consistent improvements across all tested architectures and datasets including Trackformer, MOTR, FairMOT, ByteTrack, GTR, and MOTE. Extensive evaluations show up to 53\% reduction in identity switches and 12\% IDF1 improvements across challenging benchmarks, with GTR achieving peak performance gains of 9.7\% MOTA on SportsMOT.

Rethinking Efficient Mixture-of-Experts for Remote Sensing Modality-Missing Classification

作者：Qi Gao

第一作者单位：State Key Laboratory of Integrated Ser- vice Network , Xidian University , Xi'an 710071 , China （OpenAlex first authorship）

arXiv · 2025-11-14

论文内容：论文从条件计算角度重新审视遥感多模态缺失分类，提出参数高效的Missing-aware Mixture-of-LoRAs框架，用双路由解耦共享与动态专家，实现单模型自适应多种模态缺失场景。

迁移思路：研究推断：可将双路由MoE-LoRA思想迁移到多传感器融合船舶检测/分割的融合模块，针对Radar、AIS或视觉-AIS关联中输入模态随机缺失，用模态可用性作为路由监督，解耦共享与模态感知专家，保持检测头输入结构不变并提升鲁棒性。

查看摘要（中文 / English）

多模态遥感分类常因传感器故障和环境干扰而出现模态缺失，导致性能严重下降。本文从条件计算视角重新思考缺失模态学习，并探究混合专家（MoE）模型能否内在地适应多样的模态缺失场景。我们首先系统研究了代表性MoE范式在多种缺失模态设置下的表现，揭示其潜力与局限。基于这些发现，我们提出Missing-aware Mixture-of-LoRAs（MaMOL），一种参数高效的MoE框架，将多种模态缺失情况统一于单一模型。MaMOL引入双路由机制，解耦模态不变的共享专家与模态感知的动态专家，从而根据可用模态自动激活专家。在多个遥感基准上的大量实验表明，MaMOL以极小的计算开销显著提升了多样缺失模态场景下的鲁棒性与泛化能力。在自然图像数据集上的迁移实验进一步验证了其可扩展性与跨域适用性。

Multimodal remote sensing classification often suffers from missing modalities caused by sensor failures and environmental interference, leading to severe performance degradation. In this work, we rethink missing-modality learning from a conditional computation perspective and investigate whether Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) models can inherently adapt to diverse modality-missing scenarios. We first conduct a systematic study of representative MoE paradigms under various missing-modality settings, revealing both their potential and limitations. Building on these insights, we propose a Missing-aware Mixture-of-LoRAs (MaMOL), a parameter-efficient MoE framework that unifies multiple modality-missing cases within a single model. MaMOL introduces a dual-routing mechanism to decouple modality-invariant shared experts and modality-aware dynamic experts, enabling automatic expert activation conditioned on available modalities. Extensive experiments on multiple remote sensing benchmarks demonstrate that MaMOL significantly improves robustness and generalization under diverse missing-modality scenarios with minimal computational overhead. Transfer experiments on natural image datasets further validate its scalability and cross-domain applicability.
